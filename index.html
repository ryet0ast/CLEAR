The CLEAR Framework

Craft better AI prompts step-by-step using Dr. Leo S. Lo's research-backed CLEAR Framework — a structured approach to prompt engineering.

C
Concise
Use clear, simple language. Eliminate unnecessary words so the AI focuses on what matters.
L
Logical
Structure prompts in a coherent sequence with intentional order of ideas and context.
E
Explicit
Specify format, scope, tone, reading level, persona, and examples for the output.
A
Adaptive
Remain flexible. Rephrase or decompose complex tasks as you refine your approach.
R
Reflective
Evaluate AI output for accuracy. Continuously refine based on what you learn.
Based on the research of Dr. Leo S. Lo — published in The Journal of Academic Librarianship, Vol. 49, Issue 4, 2023.
